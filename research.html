Retaining Foreign Talent: The Impact of the OPT Extension on the U.S. Labor Market and STEM Employment

Using 2012–2022 American Community Survey data and a triple-difference (difference-in-differences-in-differences) design, I examine how the 2016 Optional Practical Training (OPT) extension affects the occupational allocation of foreign-born STEM graduates in the United States. The findings indicate an increase in STEM relative to non-STEM employment among eligible graduates. The analysis also suggests a shift of U.S.-born STEM graduates toward non-STEM occupations in commuting zones with larger inflows of F-1 STEM graduates.

The Effect of the Clean Air Act on the Internal Migration of Green and Non-Green Workers

Work in progress.

Using American Community Survey and Environmental Protection Agency data, I examine how the 2015 ozone standard under the Clean Air Act relates to the geographic distribution and internal migration of workers in green and non-green occupations.
